How it works

Understanding the process

Three steps, always visible to you and your care team. Nothing happens between them that isn't explained here.

Step 1

Data Input

Your records are encrypted in transit and anonymized before Aether ever processes them.

Pattern recognition, not diagnosis

Aether flags statistical patterns worth a closer look. It does not name a condition or suggest a treatment — every flag is reviewed by a licensed clinician before anyone sees it.

Step 3

Personalized Insight

Your doctor gets a clear, reviewed summary to walk through with you — the final call is always theirs and yours.

Benefits & risks

A clear-eyed view, both ways

We'd rather you understand the trade-offs than take our word for it.

Explore the benefits

  • Tailored plans

    Recommendations shaped by your own history, not a generic protocol.

  • Early detection

    Surfaces patterns worth a second look, sooner than a routine visit might catch them.

  • Care-team alignment

    Every insight reaches your doctor alongside you, not you alone.

Understand the risks

  • Algorithmic bias

    Models trained on limited data can perform unevenly across groups. We audit quarterly and publish the results.

  • Data security

    Any connected system carries some risk. We minimize what we collect and encrypt everything we hold.

  • Over-reliance

    Aether supports your doctor's judgment. It should never replace a conversation with them.

Your data & privacy

You decide what's shared

Privacy isn't a settings page you find later — it's built into how Aether handles data from the first step.

Default

Anonymized before analysis

Identifying details are stripped before any pattern-matching happens, not after.

Your choice

Opt in to specifics

Sharing a named record with your own doctor is something you turn on, never a default.

Always

Delete on request

Ask and your data is removed from active analysis. We never sell it to advertisers.

About us

Why we're building this in the open

Project Aether started from a simple frustration: health tools that use AI rarely explain what the AI is actually doing. We think trust is earned by showing the mechanism, not hiding it — so every page on this site exists to answer a question a patient would reasonably ask.

We're a small team of clinicians, data scientists, and a dedicated Ethics Officer who has veto power over anything that ships. If something here doesn't add up, that's exactly what the contact link below is for.
